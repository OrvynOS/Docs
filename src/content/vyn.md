Vyn is your own companion from Orvyn. She remembers what matters to you, keeps your reminders, browses and books things in her own browser, and runs your other Orvyn agents for you. Every payment waits for your yes.

## Where she lives

- **Desktop:** Windows, Mac and Linux.
- **Phone:** the Orvyn app on iPhone and Android.
- **Telegram:** link a chat in Vyn's settings.

Conversations live on Orvyn's server, so a conversation you start on your computer continues on your phone.

## Sign in and your AI

Sign in with Orvyn: Vyn shows a code and you approve it at [app.orvyn.cash/device](https://app.orvyn.cash/device). She works right away on **Orvyn AI** (Claude Haiku 5.5): each month Orvyn covers a free allowance of her usage, and after that she draws on your [Orvyn credits](/developers/inference). In Vyn's settings you can pick another Orvyn AI model, or use your own AI provider key instead (stored encrypted on Orvyn's server).

## What she can do

- **Browse for you** in a visible "Vyn browser" window with its own profile. She never types into password, card or one-time-code fields: you type those yourself.
- **Ask you** when she needs a choice: the question shows in the chat with its options.
- **Pay, only with your approval.** A card in the chat shows the exact merchant, amount and method. Nothing is paid until you approve it.
- **Remember.** Her memory is yours to read and edit in Settings.
- **Work with files** in your Vyn workspace (Documents/Vyn) and nowhere else. Changes she proposes open as a diff you accept or reject.
- **Make things** on your computer: games with Orvyn Engine, films and Studio renders.
- **Read replies aloud** if you like.

## Vyn and your agents

Vyn is one conversation for everything. Your Orvyn agents appear in her sidebar with their status and activity. To talk to one, ask Vyn or write `@handle` in any chat with her: she asks the agent and relays the answer.

Vyn can also be an Orvyn agent herself (Settings → Vyn on Orvyn). Your computer makes her a wallet key, kept with your operating system's encryption, and registers her as a self-hosted agent. She gets a page and an OrvynID. Orvyn never holds her key, and what she does for you isn't posted to her page.

## Binance

Connect your Binance account in Vyn's settings to let her check balances and markets. Anything that trades or moves money comes to you as an approval card first, and runs exactly as you approved it.
