## Fair launch

- **Same terms for everyone.** Nobody gets tokens before the public, at a lower price, or on a vesting schedule. The Orvyn team buys from the pool like anyone else, if it buys at all.
- **No hidden supply.** All 1,000,000,000 tokens go to the launch. There's no reserve that could be sold later.
- **On chain.** Once launched, the supply, the pool and the tax can be checked on chain from the contract address listed here.

## Pool tax

Trades through the $ORVYN liquidity pool pay a **2% tax**. Where the tax goes will be published here before launch.

## Utility

### Discounted trading fees

Orvyn charges a fee on trades its routes make (see [Fees](/reference/fees): 0.3% on swaps). **$ORVYN holders pay a discounted fee.** Holding thresholds, discount levels and how holdings are checked will be published before the discounts go live.

### Buybacks

Orvyn will **buy back $ORVYN** from the market. How buybacks are funded, how often they run and what happens to bought-back tokens will be published here, and each buyback will be visible on chain.

## Things to know

- $ORVYN is a utility token for the Orvyn platform. It isn't a share in Orvyn, and it doesn't pay dividends or give a claim on Orvyn's revenue or assets.
- Nothing on this page is financial advice or a promise of price or returns. Token prices can fall to zero. Only buy what you can afford to lose.
- Check whether buying or holding tokens is allowed where you live.
